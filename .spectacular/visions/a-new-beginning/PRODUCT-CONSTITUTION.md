# Product Constitution — Spectacular rebuild

This is the accepted S01 contract for the `a-new-beginning` rebuild. It governs
later refactor decisions without replacing the current shipped PRD. Final public
vocabulary, artifact ontology, responsibility packaging, and implementation
architecture remain downstream decisions.

## Identity and category

Spectacular is an owner-centered governing system for substantial software
projects operating across humans, agents, tools, and sessions.

“AI-native project control plane” remains a working category proposal. S09 owns
final public language; this Constitution does not require that name.

Spectacular governs how evolving intent becomes accepted direction, bounded
accountable work, evidence-backed acceptance, reconciled truth, and durable
continuation. It does not perform or guarantee all execution inside that loop.

## Primary beneficiary

The primary beneficiary is the accountable owner of a substantial software
project—often a solo founder, technical founder, project owner, solo developer,
or member of a small software team.

The operating environment is explicitly multi-actor. Humans, agents,
specialists, and separate sessions may collaborate, but collaboration machinery
exists to protect project coherence rather than reproduce an organizational
project-management suite.

## Job-to-be-done

> Help me turn evolving ideas into maintainable, high-quality software by
> ordering the thinking, preserving shared decisions, safely distributing
> accountable work, evaluating the results, and recovering one evidence-based
> next safe action—or clearly exposing the owner decision or unknown that
> prevents one.

## Umbrella promise

> Spectacular turns evolving software intent into shared accepted direction and
> bounded accountable work, coordinating humans and agents through
> evidence-backed acceptance, reconciliation, and durable continuation so
> project owners can delegate with confidence without carrying the entire
> project in their heads.

This is a governing promise, not a guarantee that every implementation attempt
or product outcome succeeds.

## Behavioral commitments

| ID | Constitutional commitment |
|---|---|
| P1 | **Spectacular turns uncertainty into shared accepted contracts.** It governs exploration until intended outcomes, behavior, boundaries, and required evidence are explicit enough for authorized acceptance. |
| P2 | **Spectacular translates accepted product intent into an accepted, realizable direction.** It ensures material constraints, risks, technical choices, and proof strategies are addressed proportionally before implementation work is authorized. |
| P3 | **Spectacular turns outcomes into bounded, accountable work.** Each work unit preserves its authority, responsibilities, dependencies, stop conditions, expected return, and required evidence. Final work-unit names and artifact identities belong to S04/S09. |
| P4 | **Spectacular makes work safely distributable.** Humans, agents, specialists, and separate sessions can receive bounded handoffs without losing provenance, authority, or responsibility for convergence. |
| P5 | **Spectacular preserves project continuity.** A cold human or agent can recover accepted intent, decisions, current state, blockers, and the next safe action—or the unresolved gate preventing one—without reconstructing chat history. |
| P6 | **Spectacular makes completion evidence-backed.** Work is not accepted merely because an actor reports completion; evidence must address the promised behavior and declared acceptance conditions. |
| P7 | **Spectacular reconciles accepted results with project truth.** Accepted delivery updates the appropriate current contracts, decisions, status, and durable records instead of leaving implementation and governing state to drift apart. |

## Measurable aspirations

These are product hypotheses, not guaranteed outcomes:

- **P8:** Spectacular is designed to reduce the expertise and attention required
  to govern software delivery.
- **P9:** Spectacular is designed to reduce avoidable thinking fatigue,
  coordination overhead, repeated context loading, rework, and unproductive
  generation.

S02 must define cohorts, baselines, measurements, and falsification criteria.
Existing positive user feedback is supporting evidence, not comparative proof.

## Protected governing loop

```text
understand current context
→ explore consequential uncertainty
→ obtain authorized acceptance of desired behavior and boundaries
→ determine and obtain acceptance of a realizable approach
→ compile bounded accountable work
→ hand work to execution actors
→ evaluate evidence against accepted expectations
→ record authorized acceptance or rejection
→ reconcile accepted results with shared truth
→ recover one evidence-based next safe action
  or expose an unresolved owner decision or unknown
```

Final gate names, artifact types, work-unit names, and public vocabulary remain
reserved for S04 and S09.

Ceremony may compress for small, clear, reversible work. These invariants may
not disappear:

- Accepted intent and boundaries
- Declared authority and recorded authorization
- Bounded responsibility
- Traceable handoffs
- Evidence addressing promised behavior
- Reconciliation of accepted results
- Recoverable continuation
- Explicit unresolved gates when continuation is unsafe

## Constitutional capability boundary

Spectacular must provide the capabilities to:

- Ground work in accepted intent, constraints, and authority
- Classify consequential uncertainty and govern proportional exploration
- Govern the procedures through which desired behavior and realizable approaches are accepted
- Compile accepted direction into bounded accountable work
- Declare required authorization and record its source and scope
- Produce and validate handoffs across actors and sessions
- Preserve provenance and responsibility for convergence
- Define evidence expectations before work
- Evaluate returned evidence against accepted expectations
- Record acceptance, rejection, escalation, or unresolved state
- Reconcile accepted results into current project truth
- Preserve continuity and recover the next safe action or blocking unknown
- Provide deterministic validation, projection, migration, and repair support for canonical state

The Constitution requires these capabilities. It does not settle their final
packaging as Skill behavior, CLI commands, agent roles, adapters, core modules,
or optional companions. S07 owns that placement.

## Authority and permission boundary

Spectacular:

- Declares which authorization a work item or effect requires
- Records who or what supplied that authorization
- Preserves its scope, conditions, and consequences
- Prevents its own lifecycle from treating unauthorized work as accepted

Spectacular does not thereby own:

- Provider credentials
- Operating-system permissions
- Repository access controls
- Remote service authorization
- Enforcement of external effects

Execution environments and providers enforce the effects they perform.
Consequential acceptance remains with the declared authorized owner;
Spectacular governs and records the procedure but does not autonomously inherit
that authority.

## Execution boundary

Spectacular governs and compiles accountable work across the delivery loop.

It remains accountable for accepted boundaries, authorization records, bounded
handoffs, evidence requirements, return reconciliation, and durable
continuation. Execution actors remain accountable for performing their bounded
work. Native systems remain accountable for the effects they execute.
Authorized owners remain accountable for consequential decisions and acceptance.

Spectacular does not guarantee implementation success, flawless delivery, or
market outcomes.

The execution/delivery space may justify an optional companion skill that owns
a complete standalone execution workflow and returns evidence through a bounded
handoff. S07 must evaluate that possibility; S01 does not approve it.

## Git and operating-tool boundary

Spectacular requires durable, inspectable support for history, attribution,
isolation, collaboration, and recovery. Git is the best, preferred, and
initially supported baseline, but not a permanent constitutional identity
requirement.

The implementation may directly use and optimize for Git. It does not need a
speculative abstraction for hypothetical alternative substrates. GitHub or
another hosting provider is not constitutionally required.

Canonical state remains inspectable and manually repairable without
Spectacular’s official operating tools. The repository continues to function
normally.

The complete supported guarantees—guided judgment, deterministic validation,
safer mutations, lifecycle enforcement, projections, and optimized
operation—require the official judgment and deterministic-operation
capabilities, currently delivered through the Skill Core and CLI.

Manual operation is valid but slower and less protected. Independent reviewers
may deliberately inspect canonical state without invoking Spectacular to reduce
methodological bias. Final responsibility placement and packaging remain S07.

## Explicit non-goals

Spectacular is not:

- A model host or coding-agent runtime
- A generic scheduler, worker queue, or process supervisor
- An organizational project-management suite
- A Git client, Git host, or GitHub replacement
- A CI/CD or deployment platform
- A universal specialist-work suite
- A documentation wiki or public-documentation authoring system
- A substitute for code, tests, or observed runtime behavior
- The holder of provider credentials or external permissions
- An autonomous authority over consequential decisions
- A guarantee of flawless code, successful delivery, or market success
- Necessarily the executor of the work it governs
- Constitutionally tied to today’s artifact names, commands, or packaging

## Failure test

Spectacular has ceased to be Spectacular if it cannot preserve:

1. Accepted intent and boundaries
2. Bounded accountable work
3. Declared authority and recorded authorization
4. Traceable handoffs and convergence responsibility
5. Evidence-backed acceptance
6. Reconciliation with project truth
7. Durable continuation

> If Spectacular cannot preserve accepted intent, compile accountable work,
> maintain authoritative handoffs, evaluate evidence, reconcile accepted
> results, and enable safe continuation—or expose why continuation is
> unresolved—it has ceased to be Spectacular.

## Downstream constraints

- **S03A before S02:** establish minimal truth, provenance, authority, and freshness semantics before measurement.
- **S02:** define P8–P9 cohorts, baselines, falsification, and defenses against metric gaming.
- **S03B:** establish the complete truth hierarchy without making summaries or projections authoritative.
- **S04:** choose work-unit, attempt, gate, contract, and lifecycle ontology; neutral descriptions here are not final names.
- **S05:** distinguish declared authorization, recorded acceptance, owner authority, runtime authority, and provider enforcement.
- **S06:** define evidence, review independence, repair, reconciliation, retention, and safe-continuation states.
- **S07:** place capabilities across core, Skill, CLI, roles, adapters, providers, and optional companions; evaluate, but do not presume, an execution/delivery companion.
- **S08:** specify official-tool guarantees, degraded/manual behavior, inspection, repair, deterministic projections, and Git-backed retrieval.
- **S09:** select public category language and one name per accepted concept.
- **Compatibility floor before S10:** declare which interfaces, histories, and recovery paths survive.
- **S10:** judge subsystem survival against this failure test and capability boundary.
- **S11:** optimize directly for Git while keeping constitutional identity substrate-neutral; define recoverable migration and packaging.
- **S12A:** approve specifications traceable to these commitments.
- **S12B:** only afterward compile executable work with evidence and rollback traceability.
