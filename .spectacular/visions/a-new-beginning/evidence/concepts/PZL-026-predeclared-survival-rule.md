# Predeclared survival rule

## Core message

Choose subsystem survival criteria before inspecting particular candidates so
attachment and sunk-cost reasoning cannot move the threshold case by case.

## Value

Improves consistency and makes keep/remove decisions auditable.

## Assumptions

- One criterion can fairly judge safety, discovery, coordination, and UX features.
- Record counts and first-hour use represent value.

## Evidence and collisions

The proposed “three records or first-hour stranger use” threshold is unsupported
and not currently measurable. Rare preventive capabilities and recently shipped
features may be valuable without frequent records.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Anti-bias discipline versus false objectivity. Adopt the predeclaration principle,
not the numeric rule. Use multiple evidence lenses: use, uniqueness, risk reduced,
replacement cost, user evidence, and maintenance burden.
