# GH-026 — Plain-language Issue lead

## Plain-language focus

Start filed Issues with one sentence a cold reader can understand.

## Problem and evidence

Issue bodies inconsistently lead with meaning; comments do not repair the top-of-body experience.
The original reported PR pain is separately captured by #32.

## Proposed direction

Require a jargon-light lead before structured sections and enforce it at the actual filing owner.

## Relationships and collisions

Same presentation principle as #32, but the to-issues agent may live outside this repository.

## Actionability

First identify the filing skill/template owner; repo-only documentation cannot guarantee behavior.

## Refactor relevance

Supports readable handoffs but may belong to a companion capture/issue skill.
