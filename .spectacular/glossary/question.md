# Question

In Spectacular, a **question** is an unresolved uncertainty that requires an
answer before a [[capability]] or [[request]] can proceed safely. It becomes an
independent record when it needs durable tracking or blocks more than a trivial
task.

A resolved question records the answer's provenance and then leaves active
context through the lifecycle.
