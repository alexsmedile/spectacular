# Missions as bounded requests

## Core message

Rename or present a bounded, planned, owned delivery request as a Mission.

## Value

Mission conveys purpose, ownership, boundaries, and completion more vividly than
the generic word request.

## Assumptions

- Users consistently interpret Mission as one bounded execution unit.
- The term does not collide with product purpose, organizational mission, goal, or release.

## Evidence and collisions

Mission is absent from the current runtime surface; request is deeply embedded in
commands, paths, lifecycle, tests, and provider bridges. The proposed later
Operations phase may make a Mission longer-lived than a current request. Source
006 narrows Mission to an approved capability-contract delta through proof and
archive, providing a more concrete candidate lifetime but not migration evidence.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Memorable purpose versus semantic breadth and very high migration cost. Compare
an explanatory metaphor, user-facing alias, and canonical rename separately.
Mixed; the intended lifetime must be settled first.
