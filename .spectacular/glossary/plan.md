# Plan

In Spectacular, a **plan** is the durable intent of one [[request]]. It states
the request's goal, constraints, [[milestone]]s, dependencies, validation, and
deliverables.

It is narrower than a [[spec]] and must not silently redefine the approved
capability contract.
