# Artifact

In Spectacular, an **artifact** is supporting output owned by a [[spec]],
[[request]], or another durable record: for example a screenshot, benchmark,
export, prototype, or research result.

An artifact is not automatically a first-class library record. It earns an
independent record only when it needs its own lifecycle, identity, and
retrieval.
