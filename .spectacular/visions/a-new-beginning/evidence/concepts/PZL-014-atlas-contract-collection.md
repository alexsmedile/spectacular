# Atlas as contract collection

## Core message

Call the collection of capability contracts the Atlas.

## Value

Suggests a navigable map of the system rather than a pile of specification files.

## Assumptions

- Users infer “contract collection” from Atlas without translation.
- The collection represents the system landscape consistently enough for the map metaphor.

## Evidence and collisions

Atlas is absent from the current product. `specs/` is conventional and grep-safe.
The source also argues high-stakes concepts should remain neutral, while Atlas is
another metaphor that could mix space, maps, and expeditions.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Distinctive branding versus low semantic precision and substantial path/document
migration if literal. Treat as a naming candidate, not a structural proposal.
Disputed until comprehension evidence exists.
