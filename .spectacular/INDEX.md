# Durable context entry points

This is manual navigation. [index.json](index.json) is a generated governed
inventory, not a complete catalog of ordinary context.

## Start here

1. [Project](PROJECT.md): purpose, boundaries, and current direction.
2. [Ontology](ONTOLOGY.md): domain objects, terms, and relationships when needed.
3. Relevant [product need](requirements/linked-product-needs.md), then linked specs
   and delivery plans. Small plans may keep requirements inline.
4. [Current maintenance](plans/workspace-layout-cleanup.md): this layout cleanup.

## Governed and historical work

The JSON inventory resolves Contracts, Decisions, remaining Proposals, and archived
Mission bundles. Retired records live under archive/; their presence is not current
execution state. Open Proposals are P5, P10, and P12; review their remaining scope
before implementation rather than assuming every old draft is settled.

Delivered [v3 planning](archive/plans/durable-plan-mode.md) and
[v3.1 requirements work](archive/plans/linked-requirements-and-drafting.md) are
retained for their results. Raw remains an ignored free scratchpad.
