# Reduction versus planned expansion

## Core message

Evaluate planned feature work against the explicit reduction objective before
allowing it to expand the surface being simplified.

## Value

Prevents simultaneous addition and subtraction from obscuring whether the system
is becoming more coherent.

## Assumptions

- Reduction and new value cannot be combined into one smaller replacement.
- Open task count correlates with surface expansion.

## Evidence and collisions

Stance-layer and convention-pack-modules together carry 57 open top-level tasks.
Shipping them as written could contradict reduction, but either may contain ideas
that replace worse existing mechanisms.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Clear reduction signal versus freezing valuable simplification work. Review the
outcome and replacement effect, not task count alone. Strong governing question.
