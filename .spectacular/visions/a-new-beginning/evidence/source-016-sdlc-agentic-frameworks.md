# Source 016 — SDLC and agentic collaboration frameworks

## Thesis

Software work spans planning, requirements, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and
maintenance. Different delivery methods arrange those concerns differently, while agentic systems
move routine execution toward bounded workers, deterministic checks, persistent state, and
asynchronous human authority.

## Source integrity

This is a broad unsourced overview, not a comparative study or product-specific evaluation. Its
descriptions of SDLC, Agile, Waterfall, DevOps, and SRE are useful category reminders. Product and
framework claims about LangGraph, CrewAI, AutoGen, and autonomous-agent speed are time-sensitive and
were not independently verified here. They are treated as hypotheses, not adoption evidence.

Large parts restate Sources 006–010: specialist roles, deterministic gates, persistent checkpoints,
graph-shaped coordination, bounded repair, and human approval. Repetition does not increase their
evidence weight.

## Useful pressure tests

1. **Lifecycle completeness:** can Spectacular preserve accountable intent, evidence, and recovery
   across requirements, design, implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance without
   turning those concerns into mandatory sequential states?
2. **Adaptive change:** can feedback revise a Proposal or create a new delta without silently
   expanding an active Mission or invalidating its accepted baseline?
3. **Operational completion:** can a Mission declare provider-observed deployment or operational
   behavior as its completion boundary while native providers retain mutation and attestation?
4. **Multi-actor visibility:** do Mission Links, Messages, Handoffs, Run checkpoints, Gaps, and
   derived Orientation provide enough coordination without a scheduler, team-role simulation, or
   organizational project-management layer?
5. **Method neutrality:** is consequence- and reversibility-calibrated governance sufficient for
   iterative, predictive, regulated, and hybrid work, or has one delivery ideology leaked into the
   core model?

## Corrections and non-options

- SDLC concerns are coverage dimensions, not proof that Spectacular needs seven lifecycle phases.
- Agile, Waterfall, Scrum, Kanban, XP, DevOps, and SRE are not competing Spectacular product
  identities. Projects may use them while Spectacular governs accepted claims and work boundaries.
- A role diagram does not justify a permanent agent fleet. Roles should be invoked only where a
  Mission's risk and evidence contract require them.
- “Micro-sprints in seconds” is not a success metric; speed cannot offset authority, quality,
  comprehension, recovery, or maintenance regressions.
- LangGraph, CrewAI, and AutoGen are host-runtime examples, not core dependencies or architectural
  candidates without a later bounded adapter study.
