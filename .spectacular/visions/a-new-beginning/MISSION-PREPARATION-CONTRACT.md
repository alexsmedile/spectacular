# Mission Preparation Contract

## Purpose

Spectacular must govern whether an accepted direction is understood and sliced well enough to
become bounded accountable work. It does this without introducing a universal design phase,
separate Design entity, mandatory design document, or optional companion that may be bypassed.

## Core preparation loop

```text
accepted Proposal
  → candidate Mission outcome
  → candidate slices
  → Design Sufficiency check
  → Slice Quality check
  → sufficient and coherent Mission envelope
  → owner activation
  → Run
```

Material discovery during execution returns the affected path to preparation. The Run records the
discovery; existing Gaps, Decisions, Proposals, Contracts, and linked technical material retain
their own authority.

Preparation is proportional. Clear, reversible local work may compress the loop. Work involving
material scope, data, security, privacy, rights, compatibility, providers, operations, public
interfaces, or difficult reversal requires deeper evidence and review.

## Two separate verdicts

Design readiness answers whether the technical direction is understood enough to proceed safely:

```text
sufficient | needs-evidence | needs-decision
```

Slice quality answers whether the candidate Mission is a coherent accountable outcome:

```text
coherent | too-broad | fragmented | dependency-bound
```

Both verdicts must permit continuation before owner activation. A verdict never replaces its
rationale, evidence pointers, Decisions, blocking Gaps, completion boundary, stop conditions, or
authority record.

## Interaction and authority

- Guided `define` owns the adaptive judgment and candidate comparison.
- `start` mechanically validates the preparation receipt's identity, required pointers, verdict
  presence, freshness, baseline compatibility, and authorized transition. It does not decide
  semantic sufficiency.
- `resume` reopens preparation when material design, scope, risk, authority, provider, policy, or
  baseline assumptions have changed.
- The owner retains Mission-boundary and activation authority.
- Spectacular interprets evidence and specialist findings within the accepted authority envelope.
- Native providers and the host runtime retain their existing execution and effects boundaries.

Preparation is a gate within Mission definition, not a new Mission lifecycle state. No mandatory
`DESIGN.md`, durable Design record, or `designed` status is introduced. A separate design brief is
earned only when its information has an independent durable job, acceptance cycle, or freshness
boundary.

## Initial specialist roles

### Mission Slice Advisor

A constructive, option-generating advisor. It compares two or three candidate slices by observable
outcome, independent evidence, standalone coherence, dependency burden, reversibility, learning
value, integration path, and the state left behind if later work is cancelled.

It may recommend a slice. It cannot approve the Mission boundary, activate work, declare design
sufficient, schedule Missions, or turn speculative future scope into accepted truth.

### Design Sufficiency Reviewer

A skeptical reviewer of the selected slice and proposed direction. It examines Contract and
repository fit, hidden assumptions, partial failure, concurrency, reversibility, interfaces, stored
data, operational behavior, evidence adequacy, and stop or rollback conditions.

It returns supported claims, blocking and non-blocking findings, missing evidence, and bounded
corrective options. It cannot declare sufficiency, select a product trade-off, resolve the Mission,
or replace owner authority.

Independent review is risk-triggered under the accepted evidence contract. A fresh agent alone is
not independent evidence: the reviewer must not author the disputed direction, must inspect primary
evidence, and must use a distinct method, qualified person, specialist tool, or independent
observation where consequence requires it.

These are internal role profiles initially, not companion products or permanent lifecycle actors.
Extraction must satisfy the accepted S07 standalone-job test.

## Projection and acceptance

A Mission Card may project both verdicts, rationale, freshness, blocking Gaps, completion boundary,
and exactly one safe continuation or owner gate. The projection remains non-authoritative and must
drill down to its sources.

The rebuilt product must demonstrate:

- compressed preparation for a clear low-risk change;
- deep preparation for consequential work;
- reslicing after a material false assumption;
- shared-interface work across independent Missions;
- deployed and observed completion;
- incident repair through observed recovery;
- cold resume with preparation state and one safe continuation.

## Downstream constraints

- S10 evaluates current subsystem support for this behavior without prejudging survival.
- S11 defines the proportional method and deterministic validation boundary before selecting target
  modules or implementation architecture.
- S12A exposes material constraints, design Gaps, failure and interface behavior, proof, and
  operational boundaries without demanding low-level design for every change.
- S12B applies outcome, independence, coherence, evidence, cancellation-state, dependency, and join
  tests when compiling implementation Missions.

Exact schemas, files, commands, storage, triggers, and agent definitions remain downstream design
and specification work.
