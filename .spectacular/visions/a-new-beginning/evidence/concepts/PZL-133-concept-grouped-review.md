# Concept-grouped review

## Core message

Group proposals by the concept or decision they affect, preserving their source identities and
showing supporting, duplicate, dependent, and conflicting relationships.

## Value

Lets a human decide one coherent domain without rereading source-shaped backlogs.

## Assumptions

Grouping keys are explicit and do not erase provenance or minority proposals.

## Evidence and collisions

Issues #19 and #30 are already exercised by this refactor's atomic cards, domain map, conflict
matrix, and portfolio synthesis. The remaining question is which projections belong in product.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Concept grouping adds curation work and can hide nuance. Keep source cards immutable, treat group
membership as a derived index, and test filters against real decisions.
