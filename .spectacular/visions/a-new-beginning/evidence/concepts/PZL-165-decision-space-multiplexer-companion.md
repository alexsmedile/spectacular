# Decision-space multiplexer companion

## Core message

A standalone companion can turn an unresolved decision space into a tension/uncertainty map, select
the right comparative or executable artifacts, stress alternatives, and return a decision-ready brief.

## Value

Accelerates architecture, UX, workflow, and refactor choices while keeping Spectacular focused on
authority, durable decisions, lifecycle, and promotion.

## Assumptions

- The job remains coherent without `.spectacular/` and owns its artifacts behind a stable handoff.
- The human, not the multiplexer, accepts trade-offs.
- The engine can select cheaper evidence rather than always generating prototypes or agent swarms.

## Evidence and collisions

The current refactor manually exercises decomposition, contradiction mapping, option clustering, and
decision packets, providing real prior art. It has not yet proven executable artifact generation,
standalone use, namespace ownership, or maintenance cost.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Promote this to S07's leading new companion experiment. Before creating a skill, run one manual
multiplexer pass on an actual S01–S03 choice and evaluate its handoff, retained decisions, and cost.
