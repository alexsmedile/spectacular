# Retire Imagine and Vision

## Core message

Delete the Imagine/Vision lifecycle, CLI verbs, references, and doctor area because
the workflow has not produced enough durable use to justify its surface.

## Value

Would remove a large pre-request workflow and its routing/validation cost.

## Assumptions

- The observation window since shipping is sufficient.
- Vision adds no unique value beyond ideas, questions, or specs.

## Evidence and collisions

Vision shipped on 2026-08-04; this source was supplied three days later. The active
`a-new-beginning` Vision now owns this refactor's source, concept, conflict, and
decision workflow. The zero-use premise is stale and the observation window tiny.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Substantial simplification versus deleting the workflow currently demonstrating
unique value. Removal may still be considered after this refactor retrospective,
but “provably dead” is refuted.

Pending.
