# Readiness, Launch, Operations, and Closeout

## Core message

Keep solution review, release, real-world observation, and historical closeout as
distinct phases rather than collapsing them into “landing” or completion.

## Value

Prevents verification from masquerading as deployment success and preserves room
to learn from actual operation before closing the record.

## Assumptions

- These phases apply broadly enough to belong in Spectacular's universal model.
- A request or Mission remains open through production observation.

## Evidence and collisions

Current request and release lifecycles are deliberately separate: a verified
request may precede a shipped release. Libraries, local CLIs, research, and content
may have no Launch or Operations phase. Adding these as request states would be a
scope change, not terminology. Source 006 instead closes a compact mission after
proof and a review artifact, omitting Launch and Operations; the two proposed
Mission lifetimes directly differ.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Honest delivery semantics versus workflow expansion and deployment coupling.
Preserve the conceptual distinctions, then decide whether they are universal
states, optional projections, linked release records, or merely vocabulary.
Disputed as a single lifecycle chain.
