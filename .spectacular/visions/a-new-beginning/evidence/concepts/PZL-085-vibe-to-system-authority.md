# Vibe-to-system authority split

## Core message

The human owns desired experience, business/privacy rules, constraints, and non-goals;
an architect agent maps them into technical alternatives and contract deltas.

## Value

Lets nontechnical product owners exercise real authority without validating syntax or internals.

## Assumptions

- Product and engineering decisions can be separated cleanly enough for review.
- Technical recommendations remain challengeable rather than silently authoritative.

## Evidence and collisions

The vibe-to-spec attachment supports plain-language framing and human business review,
but calls the human Product Manager and Architect. Source 007 assigns technical synthesis to an agent.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Accessible product control versus over-delegated architecture. Adopt the framing split
only with explicit alternatives, provenance, and independent technical scrutiny.
