## Emerging convergence

Across otherwise different proposals, the strongest shared direction is:

- reduce universal context and empty/default structure;
- preserve a small load-bearing core;
- distinguish intent, implementation truth, observed reality, evidence, and history;
- measure retrieval and maintenance cost rather than calling the system “lean”;
- make reductions staged, reviewable, and recoverable;
- keep high-stakes contractual and authority language unambiguous;
- decide product surface before choosing implementation architecture.

This is convergence in goals, not agreement on mechanisms.

## Major unresolved contradictions

1. **Earned scaffold vs spec-first execution** — removing specs/AGENTS/POLICY from
   init may undermine current contract and safety prerequisites.
2. **One vocabulary world vs minimal hybrid** — the proposed hybrid may recreate
   the metaphor mixing it intends to solve.
3. **Usage-led deletion vs invalid units** — file, record, index, occurrence, and
   external-use counts are currently conflated.
4. **Dead Vision vs active Vision** — the workflow proposed for deletion is now
   producing the refactor evidence database.
5. **Wayfinding duplication vs differential output** — live comparison shows it
   answers a different question from status.
6. **One discovery type vs distinct authority contracts** — a human question,
   sourced fact, and authorized experiment do not currently behave alike.
7. **One request per cut vs ceremony reduction** — durable rationale is valuable,
   but exploratory decision work does not yet have approved request outcomes.
8. **Mission Control positioning vs orchestration non-goal** — the phrase may
   alter user expectations beyond naming.
9. **Line-count targets vs behavior** — 80-line skill and 6k-line Bash targets are
   hypotheses until outcome and safety metrics accompany them.
