# TASKS Review

Confirm valid flush-left checkbox syntax, at least one `### M<N>` grouping, PLAN status alignment,
`related: PLAN.md`, and no malformed checkboxes. Refine flat long lists into milestones and repair
wrong bullet syntax or frontmatter drift. Do not add, reorder, or delete the user's durable tasks.
