# Vision — a-new-beginning

> Pre-request direction spine. The agent grounds the opportunity, imagines
> concrete alternatives, and presents only the fragments needed for human
> reaction. Approval authorizes specification derivation, never implementation.

## Recovery entry points

- [`METHOD.md`](METHOD.md) — end-to-end refactor method, resume protocol, and retrospective questions.
- [`ORCHESTRATION.md`](ORCHESTRATION.md) — current program authority, handoffs, checkpoint review,
  and branch/worktree contract.
- [`FOUNDATION-PLAN.md`](FOUNDATION-PLAN.md) — four decision batches, twelve sessions, top 20
  priorities, responsibility baseline, and sequencing rules.

## Intent

Re-evaluate Spectacular's accumulated product and operational model using the
owner's drafted plans, outside suggestions, and current repository evidence.
Turn that mixed material into a coherent direction, explicit decisions,
approved specifications, and only then an executable refactor plan.

## North star

Spectacular becomes easier to understand, trust, and operate after the update:
every concept has one justified home, every workflow has a visible boundary,
and every shipped mechanism earns its maintenance cost through real user value.

## Understanding

### Current reality

Spectacular already has a broad, mature surface across its Markdown convention,
skill orchestration, Bash CLI, lifecycle records, policy gates, and agent
coordination. The current PRD, principles, decision log, system spec, code, and
tests are evidence of that system; they are not permission to preserve every
existing mechanism unchanged.

The current source set has been ingested into atomic concept cards. It contains
overlaps, contradictions, outdated assumptions, and proposals whose cost may exceed
their value; ingestion records those conditions without resolving them by default.

### Users and needs

- The project owner needs a calm, digestible review surface that supports firm
  choices without losing valuable alternatives or provenance.
- Future contributors and agents need a smaller, clearer operational model that
  can be retrieved progressively and followed without interpretive guesswork.
- Spectacular users need behavior that improves long-running project coherence
  without turning the framework into a heavyweight project-management system.

### Constraints

- Treat incoming drafts as proposals, not requirements or truth.
- Preserve contradictions and rejected paths as decision evidence; never merge
  them silently into vague compromise language.
- Keep Markdown readability, progressive disclosure, and Bash 3.2 compatibility
  as inherited constraints unless an explicit decision supersedes one of them.
- Prefer removing, merging, or simplifying mechanisms over adding parallel
  concepts, aliases, lifecycle states, or ledgers.
- Keep each artifact bounded and purpose-specific; do not create a mega-plan that
  duplicates sources, decisions, specifications, and execution tasks.
- No production refactor begins from this draft Vision. Direction approval may
  derive draft specifications; approved specifications may later seed requests.

### Material uncertainties

- Which source proposals are still relevant, mutually compatible, and aligned
  with the product's real target users?
- What should be retained, simplified, merged, renamed, deprecated, or removed?
- Which problems require product decisions, repository research, feasibility
  spikes, or direct rejection as unnecessary complexity?
- Is the update backward-compatible, intentionally breaking, or staged across
  compatibility and migration releases?
- What is the smallest coherent architecture and command/lifecycle vocabulary
  that still delivers Spectacular's core value?
- How should approved changes be divided into independent specifications and an
  ordered implementation program?

## Experience signature

Calm, legible, decisive, and low-ceremony. At any point the owner can see what a
source proposed, what conflicts with it, what remains unknown, what is currently
recommended, and which exact decision is needed next. Uncertainty is explicit;
approval is never inferred.

## Strategies considered

1. **Merge-first rewrite** — combine all drafts into one master proposal, then
   edit it down. Fast to start, but destroys provenance and encourages vague
   compromise plus speculative scope.
2. **Source-by-source adoption** — accept or reject each draft independently.
   Preserves provenance, but misses cross-cutting contradictions and can produce
   a pile of locally reasonable, globally incoherent decisions.
3. **Decision-led synthesis** — normalize each source, cluster claims by product
   theme, expose contradictions and dependencies, then decide theme by theme.
   This is the current recommendation and is captured as a pending fragment.

## Chosen direction

No product direction is chosen yet. The working recommendation is a
decision-led synthesis process; it remains pending human reaction in the Vision
manifest.

## Boundaries

In scope for this Vision:

- ingesting and reducing the supplied drafts without losing provenance;
- mapping claims to current product intent, behavior, code, tests, and decisions;
- separating settled facts, open questions, research needs, feasibility risks,
  alternatives, and actual product choices;
- recording confirmed architectural/product decisions;
- deriving small, coherent draft specifications from approved direction;
- producing an ordered, evidence-backed refactor program after spec approval.

Out of scope until explicitly promoted:

- production implementation or opportunistic cleanup;
- treating a long draft, current implementation, or agent recommendation as
  authority by itself;
- adding mechanisms solely for conceptual completeness or imagined future use;
- rewriting canonical product truth while material choices remain unresolved.
