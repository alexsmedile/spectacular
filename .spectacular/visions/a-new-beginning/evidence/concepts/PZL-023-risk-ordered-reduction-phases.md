# Risk-ordered reduction phases

## Core message

Sequence reduction as workspace hygiene, evidence-backed removal, then contested
capability decisions so early wins reduce later reasoning surface.

## Value

Lowers risk and cognitive load while producing a useful stopping point before
hard product choices.

## Assumptions

- Early work is genuinely low-risk and does not pre-decide contested outcomes.
- Later judgments benefit materially from the smaller surface.

## Evidence and collisions

The source calls sessions independent while also declaring dependencies and
possible reordering after backlog triage. The phase principle is sound; the exact
session chain is not independent.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Safer momentum versus bias toward deletion and sunk sequencing. Retain as a
workflow candidate, but let dependencies—not session numbers—set order.
