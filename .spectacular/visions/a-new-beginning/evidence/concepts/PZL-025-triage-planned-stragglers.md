# Triage planned stragglers

## Core message

Explicitly decide whether each planned request should ship, defer/park, or be
abandoned before building a reduction roadmap around it.

## Value

Makes the real backlog and future surface visible instead of letting old plans
survive by inertia.

## Assumptions

- The three requests remain the only live planned work relevant to this refactor.
- Park and abandon routes preserve necessary history and provenance.

## Evidence and collisions

The current fleet has convention-pack-modules with 37 open tasks, stance-layer
with 20 open plus 3 deferred, and commit-discipline with 3 open on hold. Shipping
the first two would expand the surface during a reduction program.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Clear scope versus risking premature cancellation before the new direction is
known. Make this an early decision domain, but compare each request against the
approved reduction goals rather than age alone.
