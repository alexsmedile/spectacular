# Product lock and engineering assurance

## Core message

Require human confirmation of behavior/business rules and an independent engineering
review of architecture, security, maintenance, complexity, and repository consistency.

## Value

Separates product authority from technical assurance without demanding founder expertise.

## Assumptions

- An independent agent provides enough diversity and evidence to block unsafe work.
- Final risk acceptance remains unambiguous.

## Evidence and collisions

Source 006 proposes one mandatory approval. Source 007 also says AI confidence is not
evidence, making “agent approval” internally ambiguous.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Stronger scrutiny versus latency and false authority. Require an independent evidence
report; keep final approval human unless a narrower mechanical gate is defined.
