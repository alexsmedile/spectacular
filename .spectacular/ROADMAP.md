# Strategic Roadmap

Macro-level product evolution and multi-horizon themes.

## Strategic Horizons

### Horizon 1: Single-Node Core & Autonomy (Current)
- **Focus**: Local-first determinism, atomic transactions, sub-second CLI performance, and Context Sandwich prompt bounding.
- **Active Campaign**: [Context Sandwich Steering](campaigns/context-sandwich-steering.md)
- **Target Outcome**: Zero-drift mechanical governance for single-agent and human developer workflows.

### Horizon 2: Multi-Agent Swarms & Isolated Worktrees (Next)
- **Focus**: Concurrent background runners, disjoint write reservations, worktree auto-pruning, and multi-model consensus.
- **Planned Campaign**: Multi-Agent Concurrency & Isolation
- **Target Outcome**: Scalable parallel execution across background agents without file collisions or authority escalation.

### Horizon 3: Enterprise Telemetry & Cryptographic Verification (Future)
- **Focus**: Cloud proof attestations, compliance audit exports, hosted telemetry, and remote review verification.
- **Planned Campaign**: Enterprise Compliance & Cloud Attestations
- **Target Outcome**: Cryptographic compliance proof verifiable in external CI/CD systems and security portals.
