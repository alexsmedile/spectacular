# AI UX stress-tester domain profile

## Core message

Specialize the decision-multiplexer workflow with AI-UX tension axes, boundary states, resilience
patterns, adversarial persona lenses, and consequence reporting before creating a separate engine.

## Value

Provides domain depth for AI interaction design while reusing general uncertainty, artifact, and
decision-brief contracts.

## Assumptions

The general engine supports domain-owned rubrics and patterns without becoming a lowest-common-denominator
framework, and repeated standalone UX use justifies packaging.

## Evidence and collisions

Source 013 supplies a coherent UX sequence but no implementation or adoption evidence. Three skills
plus an orchestrator may recreate shallow-module and context-overhead problems.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Begin as a profile/preset used on one real AI interaction. Split into an independent skill only if it
needs its own substrate, tools, lifecycle, or users beyond the general multiplexer.
