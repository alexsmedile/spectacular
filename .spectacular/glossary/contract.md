# Contract

In Spectacular, a **contract** is the reviewed agreement about a
[[capability]]: its intended outcome, boundaries, constraints, and acceptance
conditions. A [[spec]] is the current artifact that carries this contract.

The contract is durable design knowledge, not a duplicate of implementation
truth.
