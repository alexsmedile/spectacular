# Accountable advice process

## Core message

Give each consequential decision one legitimate owner who seeks advice from affected and expert
parties, records dissent and evidence, then makes or escalates the call without requiring unanimity.

## Value

Avoids both consensus paralysis and ambiguous accountability while preserving specialist input.

## Assumptions

- Authority is explicitly delegated and does not override policy, law, or another owner's domain.
- Review deadlines and escalation paths are proportional to consequence and participation needs.

## Evidence and collisions

PZL-146 separates the decider from authors and recorders; PZL-087 adds engineering assurance. Source
014 contributes a consultation protocol, but “disagree and commit” is unsafe without legitimate
authority, visible dissent, and escalation for security, privacy, or cross-domain consequences.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

One owner improves speed but can centralize bias. Adopt `owner → required advisers → deadline →
decision/rationale → dissent → escalation/appeal`, with human authority retained for value and risk.
