# Completed campaign inputs

The context-sandwich campaign and its MissionPlan inputs are retained here as
historical planning material. They do not describe active work, need ongoing
synchronization, or grant execution authority. Completed execution lives in
the archive/missions/ bundles.

The campaign map is [context-sandwich-steering.md](context-sandwich-steering.md);
its retained candidate inputs are in context-sandwich-steering-plans/. New ordinary
plans live in .spectacular/plans/; campaign structure is earned only when needed.
