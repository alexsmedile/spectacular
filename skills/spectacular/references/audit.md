# Audit

Audit one named claim or scope, not the whole workspace by default. Compile the relevant context,
then inspect its canonical sources, generation basis, fingerprints, Evidence method/freshness,
contrary evidence, Decision authority, and required review.

Report supported claims, blocking and non-blocking findings, missing Evidence, and bounded
corrective options. Do not mutate lifecycle, select a product trade-off, or equate a fresh agent
with independent Evidence. When consequence requires independence, bind a reviewer to the exact
commit/tree and give it primary Evidence plus a distinct method or cold observation. Apply the
Skill's FROST domains and return `pass | repair | owner-gate`; do not invent a comparison baseline or
repeat an unchanged review loop.

FROST means: **Frozen fit** to claim/non-goals; **Risk** to security, privacy, data, authority, and
irreversible effects; **Operability** of failure, diagnostics, recovery, and maintenance; **System
integrity** across dependencies, regressions, generation, and distribution; and **Truth of proof**
through attributable methods, freshness, contrary evidence, and no overclaim. Automatic claims use
deterministic checks, clustered claims share one domain review, and independent claims use a cold
reviewer bound to the exact tree and claim. Batch compatible reviews and reuse unchanged Evidence.
A review returns tree, claims, findings, and `pass | repair | owner-gate` without changing criteria.
