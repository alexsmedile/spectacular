## Four-scenario audit

### Fuzzy feature through maintenance

Current Capability Contracts own accepted behavior; Proposals own target deltas; Missions own
delivery; Runs own attempts; repository revisions and checks own implementation facts; providers
and observers own deployed facts; owner Decisions and authorized reconciliation own acceptance.
Material feedback stops the affected path and creates an explicit Gap, Proposal/Mission delta, or
repair Mission. Later maintenance starts from current truth without rewriting completed history.

### Regulated Mission with a false assumption

Type-1 review and the Mission authority envelope permit heavy upfront approval without claiming
the design is infallible. A material false assumption becomes a Gap and stops affected execution.
The owner may revise the accepted delta or boundary, abandon or supersede the Mission, or request
evidence. Resumption revalidates charter, baseline, policy, provider facts, and stop conditions.

### Concurrent Missions and several actors

Each Mission retains independent accountability. Typed Links express dependencies, contributions,
references, and escalation; immutable Messages carry attributable exchange without mutating the
receiver; Handoffs remain intra-Mission; pointer-first evidence remains at its natural authority.
Proposal-base mismatch and authority conflict stop reconciliation. Scheduling, staffing, and
notification delivery remain host/provider concerns rather than a new canonical progress system.

### Production incident through observed recovery

Environment observations establish what occurred; comparison with the current Capability Contract
establishes a violation; unresolved causes become Gaps; corrective work becomes a repair Mission.
Providers perform deployment or rollback and return receipts. Operational claims trigger
independent review. Closure requires observed recovery, owner disposition, and reconciliation; the
Capability Contract changes only when separately authorized.

## Owner-approved preparation-loop contract

The target behavior is:

```text
accepted Proposal
  → define candidate Mission outcome
  → generate candidate slices
  → Design Sufficiency check
  → Slice Quality check
  → sufficient + coherent Mission envelope
  → owner activation
  → Run
  → material discovery returns to preparation
  → evidence, assessment, owner disposition, reconciliation
```

This is a core, proportional judgment checkpoint embedded in guided `define`, mechanically
revalidated by `start`, and revisited by `resume` when material assumptions change. It is not a new
Mission lifecycle phase, mandatory `DESIGN.md`, separate durable Design entity, or optional
companion whose absence permits bypass.

Design readiness reports `sufficient | needs-evidence | needs-decision`. Slice quality reports
`coherent | too-broad | fragmented | dependency-bound`. Both must permit continuation before owner
activation. Simple reversible work compresses the loop; consequential work deepens it according to
scope, data, security, compatibility, provider, operational, and reversal risks.

The receipt stays pointer-first and compiles the outcome, chosen slice, approach, sufficiency and
slice verdicts with rationale, Decisions, blocking Gaps, completion boundary, stop conditions, and
evidence requirements. Existing Proposal, Mission, Decision, Gap, Contract, and linked technical
material retain their own authority. A separate Design Brief is earned only by independent durable
need.

## Owner-approved specialist slate

The owner approved two initial internal, risk-triggered specialist roles. Neither gains lifecycle,
Mission-boundary, sufficiency, acceptance, or provider authority.

### Mission Slice Advisor

A constructive advisor used early in preparation. It receives accepted outcome, relevant Contract
clauses, scope/non-goals, dependencies, Gaps, repository boundaries, completion boundary, and risk.
It returns two or three candidate slices and compares observable outcome, independent evidence,
standalone coherence, dependency burden, reversibility, learning value, cancellation state, and
integration path. It may recommend; the owner chooses and Spectacular compiles.

It runs by default for new features, broad refactors, cross-contract work, unclear boundaries,
infrastructure-first proposals, or candidate Missions with unrelated deliverables. Clear local
reversible changes may compress or skip the advisor.

### Design Sufficiency Reviewer

A skeptical reviewer used after a candidate slice and approach exist. It examines Contract fit,
repository fit, hidden assumptions, partial failure, concurrency, reversibility, interfaces, data,
operational behavior, evidence adequacy, and stop/rollback conditions. It returns supported claims,
blocking and non-blocking findings, missing evidence, and limited corrective options. Spectacular
interprets the findings; the reviewer never declares the Mission sufficient or resolves it.

Independent review is triggered for security/privacy/rights, stored data or migration, shared/public
interfaces, compatibility, multiple system boundaries, external providers, destructive or
difficult-to-reverse effects, production/observational claims, material architecture change, novel
patterns, executor-only evidence, or conflict. A fresh agent alone is not sufficient independence:
the reviewer must not author the candidate design, must inspect primary evidence, and should use a
distinct method, qualified human, specialist tool, or independent observation where consequence
requires it.

These begin as internal role profiles, not standalone companion products. Extraction remains
subject to the accepted S07 extraction test. An Operational Exposure Reviewer may later become a
risk profile of the Design Sufficiency Reviewer; it is not part of the approved initial pair.
