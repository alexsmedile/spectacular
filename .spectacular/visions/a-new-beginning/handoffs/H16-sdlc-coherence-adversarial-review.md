## Audit scenarios

Stress the accepted contracts using at least these four scenarios:

1. A fuzzy feature moves through requirements and architecture discovery, iterative implementation,
   testing, owner acceptance, deployment, observation, and a later maintenance change.
2. A regulated or high-consequence Mission requires heavy upfront approval but implementation still
   discovers a material false assumption.
3. Two independent Missions and several actors share a dependency, evidence, and escalation without
   a central scheduler or mutable inbox.
4. A production incident begins as provider/runtime evidence, becomes a contract violation and
   repair Mission, then closes only after observed recovery.

For each scenario, identify the canonical authority, owning artifact/concept, actor boundary,
transition, stop condition, evidence, and cold-resume pointer. Do not demand a named file or command
where S08/S09 deliberately left implementation details open.

## Review questions

- Does every conventional SDLC concern have a truthful route through the accepted semantic model?
- Is the model method-neutral, or does it accidentally require either Waterfall or Agile behavior?
- Can feedback change direction without silent Mission scope expansion or shadow state?
- Are deployment, operations, maintenance, and incident claims governed without Spectacular owning
  provider mutations?
- Are multi-actor coordination and safe concurrency possible without a graph runtime, role-play
  team, scheduler, WIP board, or organizational project-management surface?
- Which apparent gaps are actual missing Type-1 foundations, and which belong to S10 survival, S11
  implementation, optional companions, host runtimes, or native providers?

## Interaction rule

Begin with the audit. Ask the owner questions only if a concrete Type-1 ambiguity remains after the
four scenarios. If questions are needed, ask one tightly coupled cluster at a time with two or three
coherent options, a recommendation, consequences, and an explicit request for disposition. Stop
after each cluster and wait. Do not infer acceptance from silence or general approval.

If no owner decision is needed, return `coherent` and say so plainly. Do not manufacture a decision
session merely to justify the handoff.

- Do not choose S10 subsystem dispositions or S11 code architecture.
- Do not treat Source 016's unsourced framework/product claims as facts.
