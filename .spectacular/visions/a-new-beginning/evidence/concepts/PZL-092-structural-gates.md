# Structural gates over prompt advice

## Core message

Encode load-bearing senior behavior as checkable inputs, outputs, stops, and evidence
instead of repeating motivational instructions in every prompt.

## Value

Makes compliance observable and reduces universal prompt context.

## Assumptions

- Each behavior has a meaningful signal that resists box-checking.
- Judgment remains available where mechanical validation is insufficient.

## Evidence and collisions

The attachments mostly provide prompt advice; current Spectacular already mixes policy
directives, lifecycle gates, and doctor checks. Their effectiveness is not measured.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Reliable enforcement versus ceremony and gaming. Promote only gates tied to concrete
failure modes and validate outcomes rather than document presence.
