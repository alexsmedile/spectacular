# Working with durable context

Start from the relevant anchor, raw draft, or plan. Read linked constraints,
implement the authorized work, verify the actual change, and record useful results.
Ordinary work needs neither a Mission nor the CLI.

## Evolve the smallest useful record

Update the existing owner of a topic before creating another file. Raw can hold a
complete quick-start draft, with no metadata or promotion requirement. A maintained
plan describes an intended result, approach, progress, and outcome. Extract a
lasting explanation into Atlas or a consequential choice into Decisions when it
has an independent use.

Split by domain, code module, working phase, or delivered tranche when each part
stands alone. Keep the plan short and link those parts. A 50–120-line range is
guidance, not a limit or a reason to fragment a coherent object.

When purpose changes, an agent may move a soft document, preserve its metadata,
and repair incoming links. It should keep proposed ideas distinct from accepted
truth and resolve conflicting constraints with the owner. Routine placement does
not need a permission gate.

## Optional governed execution

Choose a named Mission explicitly when frozen scope, controlled transitions, and
formal evidence help the job. The compatible CLI checks the governed records and
performs their supported mutations. Retrieve templates from `--schema` and
validate them through the command that emits them.

Preparation establishes scope and agreement. Activation binds the Mission to its
Contract and freezes relevant authority. Execution records objectives, runs,
reviews, and evidence as required by that Mission. Completion reconciles the
outcome against the agreement; a passing test or commit alone is not formal proof
of every claim. Consult the [generated interface](../skills/spectacular/generated/mechanical-interface.md)
for the supported command sequence and exact requirements.

Contracts always keep the mechanical validation pipeline. Amend a bound Contract
through `contract amend`; changes to agreed fields require a Contract version
change. Preserve Gap entries and their resolutions. Completed Mission fingerprints
remain freeze points, even when the current Contract later changes.

Mechanical Decision validation is recommended but optional for new context.
Existing numbered, governed Decisions keep their rules. Soft Decisions use
ordinary descriptive filenames and basic metadata.

## Avoid accidental enrollment

A `.spectacular/` directory, unrelated Mission drift, or CLI absence does not
activate governance or block ordinary implementation. Without the CLI, governed
mutations are unavailable; ordinary work remains possible. Fallback inspection is
advisory and does not certify fingerprints or complete governed validation.
