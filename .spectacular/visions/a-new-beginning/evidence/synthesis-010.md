# Synthesis 010 — decision-space companion and AI UX profile

## What Source 013 adds

Source 013 extends the decision-density method with four useful mechanisms:

1. map conflicting goals before generating solutions;
2. use parametric prototypes when behavior changes across continuous boundary conditions;
3. audit AI-mediated interactions through failure consequences, fallback paths, and retained user
   control;
4. package these mechanisms as a general decision-space job rather than isolated commands.

Its AI UX patterns—intent previews, structured degradation, visible context steering, and
mixed-initiative control—are candidate domain knowledge for that job, not yet Spectacular product
requirements.

## Evidence corrections retained

- Variant and persona counts are design parameters, not success metrics.
- Synthetic personas generate hypotheses and adversarial questions; they do not constitute user
  research, accessibility validation, or behavioral evidence.
- Generated friction or trust scores are invalid unless tied to observable rubrics or measurements.
- A tension matrix exposes trade-offs but cannot make value judgments for the owner.
- Interactive prototypes are justified by dynamic uncertainty; static diagrams and prose remain
  preferable when they are cheaper and sufficient.
- A generator and evaluator built from similar models may share blind spots, so automated agreement
  is not proof.

## Companion conclusion

The owner's **decision multiplexer** hypothesis is the leading new companion experiment because it
has a plausible coherent standalone job:

> Transform a consequential unresolved decision into a mapped option space, fit-for-uncertainty
> evidence artifacts, stressed consequences, and a concise decision brief.

The responsibility boundary is:

| Owner | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Spectacular | Decision need, authoritative inputs, scope, dependencies, authority, disposition, record, and promotion |
| Decision multiplexer | Tension/uncertainty map, artifact choice, variants or harnesses, boundary stress, and consequence brief |
| AI UX profile | UX axes, failure states, resilience patterns, accessibility questions, and heuristic persona lenses |
| Specwright | Author or refine the accepted choice into a valid canonical document |
| Human | Accept the value trade-off, risk, and final disposition |

Prefer one deep general engine with domain profiles over a pack of shallow command-shaped skills.
AI UX stress testing should be the first profile. It should become a separate skill only if repeated
use proves a distinct trigger, substrate, toolchain, output contract, and standalone audience.

## Foundation Plan impact

The twelve-session order and top-20 ranking do not change. S07 now explicitly decides the
decision-multiplexer boundary and whether AI UX remains a profile. The method and session protocol
gain tension-first mapping, parametric boundary-state artifacts, and strict evidence limits for
synthetic personas.

Before skill authoring, run the multiplexer manually in a real S01–S03 decision. Judge it by retained
decision quality, reversals avoided, human attention, artifact cost, and standalone coherence—not by
the number of options or questions generated.
