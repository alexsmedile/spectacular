# Parametric boundary-state prototype

## Core message

Build one interactive sandbox that varies decision-critical dimensions and exposes zero, normal,
extreme, low-confidence, high-latency, and failure states without hand-authoring every combination.

## Value

Shows where an interaction model breaks across continuous conditions rather than only at one happy path.

## Assumptions

The selected axes represent real operating conditions and the sandbox models their visible effects faithfully.

## Evidence and collisions

This complements discrete variants in PZL-151. Too many axes create a combinatorial toy that is harder
to interpret than separate focused scenarios.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Use one to three axes and preselect meaningful boundary combinations. Record what the sandbox omits,
especially real provider latency, concurrency, accessibility, and persistence behavior.
