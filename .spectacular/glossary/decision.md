# Decision

In Spectacular, a **decision** records a durable choice and its consequences.
It earns an independent record when it constrains a [[capability]], another
[[request]], or future work. Small execution choices remain in the request
[[plan]].

A changed durable choice creates a new decision that supersedes the old one;
the historical decision is not rewritten.
