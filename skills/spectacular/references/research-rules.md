# Research Rules

Research records use canonical `RES-NNN` IDs and answer “what are the facts, evidence, and viable options?” People normally say `R1`; alternative prefixes such as `RCH`, `RSC`, `SER`, and `SRC` are not aliases. They may run AFK when read-only. Completion requires evidence plus `result: supported | refuted | inconclusive`. Only supported/refuted clears the tested uncertainty; inconclusive remains fog and needs a narrower successor. Research informs a decision but never makes one by itself. Create it only when a bounded unknown cannot be answered more cheaply from current code, tests, documentation, or direct user clarification. See [[discovery-protocol]] and [[lifecycle-contract]].

Verbs: `spectacular research new|list|resolve`. See [[canonical-ids]].
