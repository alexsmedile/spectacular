# Independent evidence review

## Core message

For consequential claims, have a context-isolated reviewer inspect the artifact and evidence
against a declared rubric rather than accepting the creator's self-assessment.

## Value

Reduces shared-context anchoring and forces claims to survive an adversarial pass.

## Assumptions

- The reviewer sees primary evidence and has authority boundaries.
- Independence means more than a different prompt over the same unsupported claims.

## Evidence and collisions

Source 008 provides no controlled evidence that parallel high-tier agents must always be
used. Agent outputs remain correlated, and reviewer confidence is not evidence. Source 006
defers multi-agent orchestration.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Fresh review improves challenge quality but increases latency and cost. Require it by risk,
claim importance, and evidence ambiguity; allow deterministic checks or humans to fill the role.
