# Synthetic persona as an adversarial lens

## Core message

Use contrasting simulated user perspectives to generate hypotheses about friction and misunderstanding,
but never treat model role-play as evidence of real users, accessibility, emotion, or trust.

## Value

Cheaply broadens pre-review questions and can expose missing states before recruiting human participants.

## Assumptions

Persona prompts are grounded in real target-user evidence and outputs are labeled as hypotheses.

## Evidence and collisions

Source 013 calls this cohort testing and proposes scores without operational definitions. Generator and
simulated-user models may share biases, hallucinate needs, and miss assistive-technology realities.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Use three to five materially different lenses to create a risk checklist. Require human research,
accessibility tooling, or observed behavior before making user claims or closing usability evidence.
