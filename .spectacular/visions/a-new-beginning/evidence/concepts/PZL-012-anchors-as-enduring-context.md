# Anchors as enduring context

## Core message

Use Anchors as the umbrella for stable project-wide grounding that constrains and
orients downstream work.

## Value

Provides a compact mental category for context that should be loaded selectively
and changed deliberately.

## Assumptions

- Every member has sufficiently similar stability and authority.
- The category remains small enough to guide retrieval rather than become “root files.”

## Evidence and collisions

Anchors is already canonical in `ARCHITECTURE.md` and `lifecycle-contract.md`.
Current membership includes more than the source's product intent, principles,
architecture, and policy. The real decision is scope and operational behavior,
not whether to invent the term.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Strong existing language with low rename cost, but a broad anchor set can falsely
imply equal authority and update cadence. Retain as a comparison baseline; review
membership alongside the minimal scaffold decision.
