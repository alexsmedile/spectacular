# Synthesis checkpoint 008

## Transition from intake to decisions

The source corpus is sufficiently complete to stop adding abstraction and begin decision sessions.
The 149 concepts reduce to four dependency-ordered batches: product constitution, operating model,
responsibility/surfaces, and reduction/delivery. Twelve sessions are small enough to focus attention
while still producing contracts rather than isolated preferences.

## Central architectural finding

Most downstream uncertainty comes from one unresolved product boundary: whether Spectacular is the
project control plane around a host coding runtime or an execution/orchestration platform itself.
The current PRD supports the control-plane interpretation. Sources 006–011 strengthen a bounded
Mission/evidence/resume loop but do not provide evidence that Spectacular should host models,
schedule concurrent agents, or duplicate provider clients.

## Responsibility finding

A minimal companion ecosystem is viable:

- pageworks is the established optional companion and boundary exemplar;
- specwright is the strongest shared-engine extraction experiment;
- bugworks is the strongest specialist-domain companion candidate;
- verifyworks and Wayfinder should wait for the Mission/evidence/discovery contracts;
- AFK should be decomposed into core authorization/resume semantics plus host/git-ops execution;
- most additional names are agent roles, Mission profiles, validators, or adapters rather than skills.

No companion is required for Spectacular's core loop, and none may mutate Spectacular lifecycle
through an implicit shared-state contract.

## Priority finding

The twenty highest-leverage decisions are not individual feature cuts. They establish product
identity, truth, work units, execution and human authority, evidence, continuity, ecosystem
boundaries, retrieval, scaffold, graph ceiling, and migration architecture. Vocabulary and subsystem
dispositions follow those foundations.
