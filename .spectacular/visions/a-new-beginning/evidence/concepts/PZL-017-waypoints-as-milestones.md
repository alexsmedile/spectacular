# Waypoints as milestones

## Core message

Call ordered, demoable mission outcomes Waypoints to emphasize navigation through
an adaptive path.

## Value

Pairs naturally with Journey and may make sequence and dependency easier to remember.

## Assumptions

- Waypoint still communicates an outcome with validation, not merely a location or checkpoint.
- The term works outside expedition/space-flavored projects.

## Evidence and collisions

Waypoint is absent from the current surface. Current milestones are explicitly
demoable outcomes and drive TASKS/verification. A rename must preserve that stronger
contract rather than weakening milestones into progress markers.

## Trade-offs and recommendation

Coherent navigation metaphor versus loss of familiar delivery language. Test the
term inside actual PLAN/TASKS examples. Promising as display language; canonical
rename remains open.
