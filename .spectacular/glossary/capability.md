# Capability

In Spectacular, a **capability** is a coherent, user-meaningful ability of the
system. It is described by an observable outcome and enduring constraints, not
by the branch, pull request, or individual change that realizes it.

A capability may be introduced or changed through multiple [[request]]s and is
usually expressed by a [[spec]].
