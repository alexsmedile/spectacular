# Synthesis checkpoint 009

## What Source 012 adds

Source 012 contributes a decision-interface layer: use comparative or executable artifacts when
they expose coupled uncertainty more faithfully than prose. Its useful unit is not a five-skill
pack; it is an artifact-selection rule spanning decision sessions, discovery, and verification.

Seven new concepts capture the non-duplicate contribution:

- validated decision density rather than raw choice count;
- deliberately contrastive functional variants;
- branch-first optioned grilling;
- disposable executable logic/state harnesses;
- bounded pre-human quality gates;
- uncertainty-shaped artifact routing;
- capability composition before skill extraction.

## Corrections that preserve decision quality

High density is useful only when decisions are understood, retained, and not reversed because the
surface hid consequences. The exact 10–20 figures are unsupported. Three variants, one question per
turn, 100% alignment, and “critic until satisfied” are tactics or absolutes, not safe universal
contracts.

The corrected operating rules are:

1. batch coupled experiential choices that benefit from side-by-side comparison;
2. traverse dependent foundational choices sequentially because each answer changes the next branch;
3. select artifact fidelity from uncertainty type, consequence, reversibility, and cost;
4. separate defect removal from human-owned product decisions;
5. bound every evaluator/repair loop with explicit terminal states;
6. classify capabilities as skills only after standalone job and substrate tests pass.

## Foundation Plan impact

The four batches, twelve sessions, and top-20 ordering remain unchanged. Source 012 refines their
method:

- S01 gains validated decision density as a secondary, guarded metric.
- S06 evaluates a bounded pre-human evidence gate.
- S07 classifies the proposed five commands by responsibility rather than naming.
- All sessions now choose the cheapest sufficient interaction artifact before questioning.

The source does not justify a Decision Density Skill Pack, universal multi-agent Gauntlet, or
separate prototype/grooming/harness/gauntlet/wayfinder skills.
