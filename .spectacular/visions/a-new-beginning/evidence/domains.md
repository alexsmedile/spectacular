## Current decision frontier

The dependency-ready decisions are not “what do we delete?” They are:

1. Decide whether to authorize a small pre-refactor stabilization request for
   PZL-047 and PZL-048 without adopting the rest of Source 005.
2. After intake closes, decide whether Spectacular owns autonomous execution,
   persists a host-owned Mission contract, or remains a lifecycle control layer.
3. Define accepted contract truth versus implementation and observed reality.
4. Resolve the Mission identity fork: bounded request, portfolio goal, or both with an explicit
   relationship; jointly grill PZL-134 and PZL-139 before binding graph views.
5. Select graph adoption Level 0–3 using synthesis checkpoints 003–007; Sources
   008–011 do not justify advancing beyond a measured Level-1 experiment.
6. In S01, define the product identity and protected core behaviorally.
7. In S02, define a normalized evidence and survival rubric against that identity.
8. In S02, decide whether this refactor optimizes primarily for adoption simplicity,
   agent retrieval cost, maintainer cost, or a weighted combination.
9. In S02, decide whether validated decision density belongs in that weighted scorecard and how
   retention, reversal, fatigue, and artifact cost prevent it from becoming a gameable count.
10. Use one real S01–S03 decision as a manual multiplexer experiment, then bring its measured
    standalone value, handoff quality, and artifact cost into S07's companion decision. Include
    impact, reversibility, uncertainty, and evidence threshold in its intake packet.

Once those are explicit, backlog triage, skill/scaffold design, vocabulary testing,
and subsystem survival can be evaluated consistently.
